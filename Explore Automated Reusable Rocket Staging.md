# Explore Automated Reusable Rocket Staging

## Future Concept

Multi-stage Rockets and detachable reusable boosters are a possible post-Alpha expansion. Alpha uses one main Rocket stage.

## Durable Requirement

- Staging must fit a technology and automation-focused modpack rather than create repeated manual recovery work.
- A reusable design must support automation of launch, booster separation, booster return, recovery, refueling or reloading, reintegration with the main vehicle, and subsequent relaunch.
- The exact staging, recovery, failure, and assembly model remains intentionally undefined until this future system is designed.

In this possible post-Alpha expansion, players would automate the cycle from launch and booster separation through recovery, servicing, reintegration and relaunch. The specific staging and recovery mechanics remain undefined.
