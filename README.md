# Galaxia Design Notes

Open [Galaxia Design](00%20-%20Galaxia%20Alpha%20Command%20Center.md) to browse by system, or [Alpha Design Map](Galaxia%20Alpha%20Wayfinder.md) for open design packages. The repository can also be opened as an Obsidian vault.

Notes describe gameplay rules, recipes and design choices. Settled rules appear first, proposals and open questions remain marked, and **Gameplay Flow** explains how the mechanics fit together from the player's perspective. A resolved design is not a claim that the feature is implemented.
