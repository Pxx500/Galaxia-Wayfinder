# Define Asteroid Automation Contract

## Question

What complete Alpha contract governs persistent Star Map asteroid discovery, eligibility for Automated Outposts, extraction, effectively inexhaustible resources, vehicle traffic, logistics, upkeep, and the separation from local asteroid terrain inside Regional Dimensions?

## Resolution

Persistent Asteroids use the accepted discovery model and zero-gravity Automated Outposts, not player-visitable terrain or Automated Stations. Multiple independent Outposts may extract effectively inexhaustible resources from one asteroid. Construction-supply delivery can precede a Dock, while normal servicing requires its ordinary infrastructure. Shared facility upkeep and transport rules apply without an asteroid-only system.

This closes the asteroid-specific planning contract from existing owner decisions. Numerical generation, throughput, and cost values remain tuning, and missing implementation is not evidence that those decisions are unanswered.

## Accepted Construction and Multiplicity Rules

- A Construction Site for an asteroid Automated Outpost can receive construction materials from a Rocket or Spaceship before an ordinary Dock is built.
- This initial delivery capability is limited to construction supply. It does not provide the refueling, ordinary cargo handling, or other services of a completed Dock.
- This capability does not authorize Hammer delivery without a receiving installation. Hammers retain their receiver requirement.
- One Persistent Asteroid may host multiple independent Automated Outposts. There is no arbitrary one-Outpost-per-asteroid cap in the current scope.
- Each Outpost has its own modules, its own shared facility inventory, and its own logistics configuration. Multiple Outposts do not imply a shared asteroid-wide inventory.
- With effectively inexhaustible resources, the player may scale extraction on one suitable asteroid by adding Outposts. Different resource composition and location can motivate using other asteroids, rather than a mandatory relocation rule.

## Provenance

The owner accepted both construction-supply and multiple-Outpost recommendations in the Wayfinder conversation on 2026-09-10.

## Remaining Scope

The common capability relationship is resolved. Numerical orbit criteria for other bodies do not block the already accepted asteroid Outpost model. These decisions do not define numerical resource generation or balance coefficients.
