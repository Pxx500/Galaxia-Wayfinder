# Galaxia Theia Design Fragments Research

> Historical or reference record. Read dated outcomes and later corrections before using earlier proposals. This record is not an active instruction to execute its plan, and its code or verification claims have not been revalidated by the 2026-09-10 vault consolidation.

## Provenance and Current Boundary

Source: [[Discord Source - Theia Design Fragments]]  
Original: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1QqSLPTlDLzuH3GmzAsWiRg41ZnFbkpdeJxYa4-w1fVE/edit?tab=t.0

The source contains isolated ideas for `Theia`, the retired working name for the Moon in the Vael destination design. The responsible developer is inactive. Current Wayfinder work therefore uses the GTNH destination baseline and preserves `Overworld -> Moon -> Mars`; it does not adopt the Theia name, lore, or mechanics until that developer returns and confirms them.

The Moon remains an Alpha Progression-Bearing Destination because of the accepted GTNH baseline. These fragments do not define its Alpha contract. Ore placement remains governed by the maintained Ore Sheet.

## Superseded or Rejected Uses

- Do not restore `Theia` as the current destination name.
- Do not substitute a Vael-specific Moon progression for the accepted GTNH `Overworld -> Moon -> Mars` baseline.
- Do not treat any listed environmental factor as an Alpha requirement merely because it appears in this fragment.
- Do not infer concrete magic, contamination, Blood Moon, magnetic-field, dust, or research systems from single-line prompts.
- Do not derive Moon ore placement from the impact-deposit idea; use the Ore Sheet for progression availability.

## Corroborated System-Level Inputs

The source independently supports three already useful system directions without accepting its destination design:

1. Celestial properties such as gravity, radiation exposure, magnetic fields, and surface material should be authored data only when they grant or constrain understandable capabilities.
2. Destination terrain should provide object-native interaction rather than act as decoration.
3. Craters may own localized finite deposits, subject to the unresolved Crater Nodes contract and Ore Sheet compatibility.

## Questions Requiring the Responsible Designer

1. Is `Theia` still intended as more than an obsolete name for the Moon?
2. Which one or two themes are actually central to the Moon: desolation, preserved history, magic, magnetism, dust, or contamination?
3. What concrete player action consumes each selected theme, and what usable result does it produce?
4. Is any proposed activity required for the Moon's Ore-Backed Tier Gate, optional depth, or post-Alpha content?
5. Are crater deposits part of the general Crater Nodes system or a distinct Moon-only mechanic?
6. Do `Moon bugs` and `Blood Moon` still represent intended mechanics, and what do those terms mean?

## Alpha Disposition

- **Moon:** remains in Alpha through the authoritative GTNH destination baseline.
- **Theia identity and bespoke mechanics:** deferred until the inactive owner returns or the project owner explicitly reassigns the destination design.
- **Reusable environmental-property and crater concepts:** route to their existing system tickets without importing Theia lore or declaring Moon-specific requirements.
