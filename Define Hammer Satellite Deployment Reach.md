# Define Hammer Satellite Deployment Reach

## Question

From which environments can a Hammer deploy satellites, and which destination orbits can local and Big Hammers reach?

## Resolution

On 2026-10-07 the owner accepted the following satellite-deployment rules:

- A Hammer on a body's surface can deploy satellites when that body has no atmosphere. An atmosphere prevents surface Hammer deployment.
- A local Hammer on an orbit deploys satellites only into that same orbit. Local deployment from an atmosphere-free surface targets that body's orbit.
- A Big Hammer can deploy satellites to supported orbits throughout the same star system, including orbits of different planets and moons. It cannot deploy into another star system. The surface atmosphere restriction also applies to Big Hammers.

Here, a star system means the whole system such as the Solar System, not only one planet and its moons. The earlier system-wide Big Hammer proposal is now accepted gameplay intent.

The accepted `Deploy to Orbit` operation targets an orbit without a cargo receiver, as described in [[Define Satellite Manufacturing and Deployment Progression]]. These rules resolve deployment origin and reach. Exact energy costs, recipes and numerical balance remain separate content work. Resolution does not establish implemented behavior.

## Provenance

Direct owner answers in the Wayfinder conversation on 2026-10-07. The owner allowed surface deployment without atmosphere, accepted same-orbit local deployment, and confirmed whole-star-system Big Hammer reach after clarifying the distinction from a planet and its moons.
