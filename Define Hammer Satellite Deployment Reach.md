# Define Hammer Satellite Deployment Reach

## Accepted Decision

| Launch environment and Hammer | Supported deployment |
| --- | --- |
| Atmosphere-free surface, local Hammer | That body's orbit |
| Orbit, local Hammer | The same orbit only |
| Atmosphere-free surface or orbit, Big Hammer | Supported planet/moon orbits throughout the same whole star system |
| Any atmospheric surface | No Hammer satellite deployment |
| Another star system | No Big Hammer deployment |

A star system means the whole Solar System, not just one planet and its moons. The surface restriction applies to Big Hammers too.

The explicit `Deploy to Orbit` mode needs no destination cargo receiver under [[Define Satellite Manufacturing and Deployment Progression]]. This is a deployment rule, not ordinary freight's atmospheric-density limit or a general receiver-free cargo route.

Exact energy costs, recipes and numerical balance remain content work. Accepted reach does not establish implementation.

## Provenance

Direct owner answers on 2026-10-07 accept atmosphere-free surface deployment, local same-orbit reach and whole-star-system Big Hammer reach.
