# Map Alpha Tier Routes

Each Ore Tier from 1 through 10 needs at least one Valid Tier Route through retained destinations, capabilities, activities and rewards, preserving meaningful alternatives and verified GTNH progression gates. This map remains open and blocked on [[Verify GTNH Ore Tier Gates]].

## Early Route Working Outline

This outline is partial, not verified complete Alpha progression. The GTNH evidence describes the original game, not automatically Galaxia policy.

| Step | GTNH 2.8.0 evidence | Accepted Galaxia direction | Still needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overworld to Luna | Ordinary late-HV Moon access precedes the EV material step | Observation-satellite deployment opens the Star Map, then vehicle capabilities determine access to Luna | Concrete early Rocket parts, costs, and route balance |
| Lunar activity | Bauxite or ilmenite provides titanium feedstock, with further chemical and machine processing required | Preserve the titanium chain. Lunar craters and Space-Grade Alloy recipes follow [[Design Lunar Crater Processing Recipe Chain]] | Validate accepted initial Sc/extractor settings, complete extractor geometry and integration, and verify throughput and downstream uses |

Physical capability replaces a Rocket-tier destination whitelist | Which Galaxia parts and technology recipes preserve the intended material progression |
| Continue to Mars | Original route includes a lunar dungeon schematic and Tier 2 Rocket access | Planetary bosses and dungeons are optional, not mandatory progression gates | A concrete vehicle and supply chain that can perform the trip without a hidden progression bypass |

The first Physical Station's construction flow and candidate production/refueling roles are recorded in Define First Physical Station Contract. Its exact position in this route remains dependent on concrete recipes and flight balance. Do not add a separate station-completion flag to this outline.

Several blueprints may use materials from the same planet, and one blueprint may unlock several related recipes. These content groupings must be chosen from actual technologies rather than mechanically matching one blueprint to one planet or one item.

## Open Route Work

Review complete route groups rather than individual ores: early expansion through Luna and Mars, industrial solar-system expansion through asteroids and inner planets, outer-system progression, and extrasolar completion. These are planning batches, not runtime destination roles or prescribed itineraries. Confirm their boundaries against the actual material chains.

Each batch must show accessible sources, processing, required non-ore feedstocks, transport, persistent infrastructure and the downstream consumer. Reuse accepted mechanics and the complete backfill contract where a new destination-specific system would add little. Existing outer-system and extrasolar research needs classification before claiming those routes verified.

- Assign concrete technologies and parts to the accepted material roles without circular access requirements.
- [[Define First Coolant Engine Production Stage]] owns the accepted resource-relative introduction stage. Concrete recipes must realize that ordering without treating LuV as a separately accepted requirement.
- Realize orbital control-electronics access before Luna without requiring it for manual lunar travel, under [[Specify Alpha Opening Mission Flow]]. The candidate mandatory refueling example remains unapproved.
- Complete later Ore Tier portfolios and preserve verified material-processing dependencies and meaningful alternatives.
- Choose a source of each required material, including allowable alternative sources, before validating full route balance.

Exact quantities, planetary masses, engine performance, and statistically safe mission limits need implementation-time measurements. They are not a reason to reopen accepted qualitative rules.

[[Define Alpha Transport Technology Baseline]] owns accepted introduction stages, Hammer tier behavior, desh parts, Callisto coolant direction, the nearby asteroid alternative and later europium requirement. [[Define Alpha Technology Knowledge Contract]] owns blueprint acquisition and production checks. [[Verify GTNH Ore Tier Gates]] records version-specific original GTNH evidence. Original Rocket tiers and recipes do not automatically become Galaxia policy, and the all-tier route cannot close before that research prerequisite is complete.

## Gameplay Flow

The accepted opening starts with satellite deployment to open the Star Map, followed by a capability-based journey to Luna. The route under design then connects lunar extraction and material processing to improved vehicle parts and travel to Mars, without a mandatory dungeon or boss gate. The bauxite, ilmenite and plating entries above are evidence from original GTNH for selecting that chain, not a completed Galaxia route.

The concrete vehicle and supply chain, later routes and their balance remain open. Each route needs accessible resources, processing, transport, persistent infrastructure and a downstream use.
