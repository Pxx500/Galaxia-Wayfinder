# Map Alpha Tier Routes

## Question

For each Ore Tier from 1 through 10, which retained destinations, prerequisite capabilities, activities, and rewards form at least one Valid Tier Route while preserving meaningful alternatives and every verified GTNH progression gate?

## Early Route Working Outline

Partial progress as of 2026-09-11. This is a working route outline, not a verified complete Alpha progression or final recipe assignment. Verify GTNH Ore Tier Gates owns the version-pinned evidence.

| Step | GTNH 2.8.0 evidence | Accepted Galaxia direction | Still needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overworld to Luna | Ordinary late-HV Moon access precedes the EV material step | Observation-satellite deployment opens the Star Map, then vehicle capabilities determine access to Luna | Concrete early Rocket parts, costs, and route balance |
| Lunar activity | Bauxite or ilmenite provides titanium feedstock, with further chemical and machine processing required | Preserve material-backed progression and the existing titanium-chain direction. Planet-unlocked materials can supply technology-blueprint recipes | Reconciled lunar extraction and Space-Grade Alloy content |
| Develop later vehicle parts | Tier 2 plating uses meteoric iron and pre-Mars tungstensteel, with EV manufacturing requirements | A Technology Blueprint can unlock a related group of part recipes. Physical capability replaces a Rocket-tier destination whitelist | Which Galaxia parts and technology recipes preserve the intended material progression |
| Continue to Mars | Original route includes a lunar dungeon schematic and Tier 2 Rocket access | Planetary bosses and dungeons are optional, not mandatory progression gates | A concrete vehicle and supply chain that can perform the trip without a hidden progression bypass |

The first Physical Station's construction flow and candidate production/refueling roles are recorded in Define First Physical Station Contract. Its exact position in this route remains dependent on concrete recipes and flight balance. Do not add a separate station-completion flag to this outline.

Several blueprints may use materials from the same planet, and one blueprint may unlock several related recipes. These content groupings must be chosen from actual technologies rather than mechanically matching one blueprint to one planet or one item.

## Remaining Route Decisions

- Assign concrete technologies and parts to the accepted material roles without circular access requirements.
- [[Define First Coolant Engine Production Stage]] owns the accepted resource-relative introduction stage. Concrete recipes must realize that ordering without treating LuV as a separately accepted requirement.
- Establish the required Physical Station role and its place in the route without treating a candidate refueling example as approved.
- Complete later Ore Tier portfolios and preserve verified material-processing dependencies and meaningful alternatives.
- Choose a source of each required material, including allowable alternative sources, before validating full route balance.

Exact quantities, planetary masses, engine performance, and statistically safe mission limits need implementation-time measurements. They are not a reason to reopen accepted qualitative rules.

The all-tier route cannot be closed before its research prerequisite is complete. Accepted partial baselines are stored in their separate resolved decisions rather than working this blocked ticket as an improvised interview queue.
