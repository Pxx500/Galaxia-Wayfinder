# Define Non-Material Tier Gate Role

## Question

What role can knowledge, research, or data play inside a Closed Tier Cycle while preserving acquisition and processing of the tier's progression material as the hard gate?

## Resolution

- Every Ore Tier in the current Alpha model uses an [[CONTEXT#Ore-Backed Tier Gate|Ore-Backed Tier Gate]]. Acquiring the intended off-world progression material and processing it is the tier's primary non-substitutable requirement.
- Research, knowledge, and data are supplemental gameplay mechanics. They may unlock a destination, expose or advance an investigation, enable an activity or capability, or otherwise participate inside the Closed Tier Cycle.
- Alpha does not currently require a research, knowledge, or data result in every tier, and no research-only or data-only tier is planned.
- A Valid Tier Route may vary the destinations and activities used to reach the material gate, but it may not replace the required material with a result from a later tier or remove an Ore Tier from the progression chain.
- Alpha should stay relatively close to the Galacticraft progression baseline where Galaxia lacks a confident replacement design. Additional research or data mechanics should be added when their gameplay purpose is understood, not invented merely to make a Tier Gate composite.
- This is a working Alpha default rather than an immutable rule. A compelling future tier concept may reopen the decision, but any exception must be explicit and must preserve the sequential progression backbone.

Conceptually, research may surround the material gate without replacing it:

`prior capability -> research or investigation activity -> acquire progression material -> process material -> satisfy Tier Gate`

The research or investigation step is optional at the system level: a concrete tier may require it, but the Alpha-wide rule does not.

## Bosses and Dungeons

Planetary bosses and dungeons are not mandatory progression gates in Galaxia. They need not exist on every destination and may remain optional content on selected objects. Required progression must be completable without defeating a boss or clearing a dungeon.

In particular, a dungeon-exclusive Rocket schematic must not block the player from progressing through otherwise available materials, production capabilities, and vehicle construction. Any optional boss or dungeon reward must not be the sole available source of a mandatory progression item or unlock.

This does not prohibit optional exploration or combat rewards and does not declare that every dungeon will be removed. Their locations and reward designs remain separate content decisions.

Owner clarification on 2026-09-11 supersedes carrying over Galacticraft's mandatory lunar dungeon schematic gate. Historical GTNH source notes retain their descriptions of the original modpack, not the Galaxia target.

The owner subsequently proposed planetary research that unlocks better technology as the replacement direction. Define Alpha Research and Production Floor owns the still-open form and scope of that system. Removing the dungeon gate does not mean that every technology must be craftable without research.

For Alpha, the owner then selected crafted technology blueprints using material unlocked through the relevant planet. Advanced planetary research is deferred. Define Alpha Research and Production Floor owns this minimal blueprint contract and its remaining use semantics.

## Consequences

- Do not pre-assign a mandatory Composite Tier Gate merely to prove that Alpha contains non-extraction gameplay.
- Chart research and data mechanics only when a concrete destination, activity, or progression purpose makes their question specific enough to ticket.
- Treat any proposal for a research-only, data-only, or non-material-substituting tier as a future exception requiring a new decision.

## Remaining Fog

- Which concrete tiers contain research or data activities and what purpose each activity serves.
- Whether a concrete research step is required before destination access, during destination investigation, or before using a discovered capability.
- How research results are represented, transported, processed, consumed, and communicated once a specific mechanic exists.
