# Define Non-Material Tier Gate Role


## Question

How can research, knowledge and data participate in a Closed Tier Cycle without replacing acquisition and processing of its progression material?

## Resolution

- Every current Alpha Ore Tier has an [[CONTEXT|Ore-Backed Tier Gate]]. Acquiring and processing the intended off-world material is the primary non-substitutable requirement.
- Research, knowledge and data may unlock access, advance investigation or enable activities and capabilities within that cycle.
- Not every tier needs such a mechanic. No research-only or data-only tier is planned.
- A Valid Tier Route may vary destinations and activities but cannot remove an Ore Tier or substitute later-tier results for its required material.
- Where no confident replacement exists, stay near the Galacticraft progression baseline rather than invent research merely to make gates composite.
- This is an Alpha default. An explicit future exception must preserve the sequential progression backbone.

A concrete tier may require research before access or production, but Alpha does not require a research step in every tier.

## Bosses, Dungeons and Technology

Bosses and dungeons are optional. Required progression must be completable without them, and no mandatory item or unlock may have a dungeon-exclusive or boss-exclusive source. This does not require removing every dungeon or optional combat reward.

Crafted Technology Blueprints are the accepted Alpha acquisition mechanism. [[Define Alpha Technology Knowledge Contract]] owns activation, consumption, team knowledge and production checks. Richer planetary research is deferred. Removing mandatory dungeon schematics does not remove all knowledge requirements.

## Open Questions

- Which concrete tiers use research or data, and for what purpose
- Where those activities sit relative to destination access, investigation and capability use
- Representation, transport, processing, consumption and presentation once a concrete mechanic is selected
