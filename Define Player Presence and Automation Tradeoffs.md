# Player Presence and Automation Tradeoffs

Resolved: 2026-07-31


## Presence and Automation

- Presence is an activity-level rule, not a universal Ore Tier or destination requirement.
- Some activities are player-bound because interaction is the gameplay. The proposed biological destination illustrates exploration, flora gathering, fauna encounters and field research, but its actual content remains unreviewed.
- Standard extraction and other activities may become automatable after their prerequisites.
- Automated Facilities are not available from the start. [[Define Automated Facility Introduction]] sets EV as the working target through construction prerequisites, not a player-tier check. Ordinary physical-machine automation during MV or HV remains allowed.
- Manual routes spend player time, attention, travel and risk. Automation saves time and attention but needs meaningful costs, constraints or risks.
- Candidate costs include construction capital, module-specific upkeep, supply logistics, energy and throughput limits.
- Alternatives need understandable advantages and disadvantages, not identical numerical strength. Automation is part of the Progression Ecosystem, not a universally superior replacement.

## Upkeep

[[Define Automated Facility Upkeep Behavior]] owns the accepted rule: actual work incurs upkeep. Missing supplies temporarily stop only the affected module with a warning. Existence and idle time do not consume upkeep.

Resource choices, cadence, buffering and numerical balance remain module-specific. Earlier per-minute or per-hour examples are not accepted tuning.

## Terminology

Use Automated Facility for general discussion. Automated Outpost means body- or surface-anchored placement and may support local mining. Automated Station means orbital placement and cannot mine the underlying body.

Normalize casual dictated Station/Outpost usage to the umbrella term unless placement or capability is material. Ask only when that distinction changes a consequential decision and cannot be inferred. Orbital processing, energy, data, storage or transport support does not give a Station direct surface mining.

## Gameplay Flow

The player can perform activities personally, spending time on travel and the risks of being present. Where automation is available, they can instead build and supply infrastructure to save attention, with its own costs and limits. Ordinary physical-machine automation can begin before the working EV target for Automated Facilities.
