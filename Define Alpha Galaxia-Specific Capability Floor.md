# Alpha Galaxia-Specific Capability Floor

Alpha needs a complete playable path through the retained GTNH progression to its existing endpoint, alongside a yet-to-be-selected floor of Galaxia-specific capabilities. This ticket remains open.

## Progression and Scope

Basic travel, resource acquisition, processing and vehicle progression may initially resemble the existing loop. Deeper Galaxia gameplay can replace stretches later. Neither elaborate chains nor a bespoke mechanic on every destination is required for the first complete path. Existing selected features retain their scope, including the ban on early teleportation bypasses.

Define the Alpha floor by complete capabilities and their gameplay chains, not a fixed number or percentage of redesigned destinations. Reuse or expand persistent transport and production infrastructure across tiers instead of requiring a new installation at each tier. Retained destinations may use [[Define Galacticraft-Equivalent Destination Backfill|complete backfill]] where appropriate. Luna industry is an initial milestone, not the endpoint.

Restrictions must follow understandable, consistent conditions. One accessible location may be the only suitable one, but a second location that meets the same conditions must be treated consistently. Do not invent alternative routes as a quota or use an exact pressure or similar value solely as a disguised planet identity check.

Remote-controlled android bodies are accepted Alpha scope under [[Define Remote Android Body Lifecycle]]. Their core gameplay and operator/body architecture are accepted, while production recipes and verification remain outstanding. Rocket missions and androids retain their existing scope without a redesign here.

## Distributed Industry

The intended industrial network asks players where to manufacture, what to transport and which persistent installation to expand. Environmental production, extraction economics, power and communication give locations practical value. These are design principles, not destination categories stored in code.

The outcomes below organize candidate integration work. They do not approve every proposed data-processing or power detail:

| Complete player outcome | Decision owner |
| --- | --- |
| Deploy the opener, discover the system, perform an initial lunar mission and return usable resources | [[Specify Alpha Opening Mission Flow]] |
| Establish a working microgravity workshop and manufacture electronics and satellite materials from obtainable feedstock | [[Define First Physical Station Contract]], [[Design Early Orbital Production Recipe Chain]] |
| Extract crater resources, process them, recover reagents and supply useful construction or equipment | [[Design Lunar Crater Processing Recipe Chain]] |
| Keep remote factories supplied with physical freight and viable power | [[Require Explicit Interplanetary Logistics]], [[Define Space Factory Power Supply]] |
| Use communication and processed prospecting for actual remote operation and extraction benefits | [[Define Remote Android Body Lifecycle]], [[Define Prospecting Data Processing]] |
| Continue through every retained tier with reachable sources, processing, transport and downstream consumers | [[Map Alpha Tier Routes]] |

## Open Decisions

Select and approve the mandatory Galaxia-specific capabilities, distinguishing product identity from optional or post-Alpha depth. For each, identify its inputs, player decisions, consequences, progression continuation, destinations and [[Define Closed Tier Cycle|Closed Tier Cycles]]. Capabilities outside that list may be deferred or supplied through a [[Define Minimum Galacticraft-Equivalent Destination Contract|Galacticraft-equivalent destination]] when progression remains complete. Individual contracts are substantially specified, but release closure and remaining service and content choices still need review. Preserve topic-specific dependencies rather than adding a blanket consultation blocker.

Modular satellites, richer research experiments and new room-discovery algorithms remain deferred. Bounded prospecting consumers and the detailed service economy still require approval. The imported proposals have been classified in their review tickets, but their authorship alone does not approve them.

## Gameplay Flow

The player deploys the opener, travels to Luna and returns usable resources, then continues through the retained progression using accessible sources, processing and transport. The complete set of required chains remains open.

The industrial direction lets players decide where to manufacture and which installations to expand, then supply remote factories with physical freight and power. More elaborate destination industry can deepen the route after the complete progression path is available.
