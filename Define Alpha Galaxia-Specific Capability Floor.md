# Define Alpha Galaxia-Specific Capability Floor

## Question

Which Galaxia-specific capabilities and complete gameplay chains belong in Alpha while first restoring a complete playable path through the retained GTNH progression?

## Provisional Direction

- Prefer a capability-based Alpha floor over a fixed number or percentage of redesigned planets.
- Require the systems that define Galaxia's product identity to operate in complete gameplay chains; let the number of destinations that must be redesigned emerge from those requirements.
- Do not use the amount of custom celestial content as a proxy for product completeness.

## Accepted Delivery Priority

First provide a complete path through the retained GTNH progression to the same end point. Basic travel, resource acquisition, processing and the next vehicle capability may initially resemble the existing progression loop. Once that path works, replace individual stretches with deeper Galaxia gameplay. Elaborate chains and a distinct mechanic on every destination are not prerequisites for this first complete path.

Existing explicitly selected Galaxia features retain their scope. This priority does not authorize early teleportation bypasses or cancel those features. Reuse persistent transport and production infrastructure instead of inventing another mandatory installation at every tier.

Restrictions must follow understandable, consistent rules. A process may have only one currently accessible suitable location. There is no requirement to manufacture multiple routes. Avoid exact pressure or similar authored values used solely as disguised planet identity checks. If another accessible location really satisfies the same process conditions, the rule must treat it consistently.

## Accepted Individual Scope Decisions

- [[Define Remote Android Body Lifecycle]] establishes remote-controlled android bodies as Alpha scope. Core gameplay and the operator/body architecture are accepted. Production recipes and verification remain outstanding.

## Industrial Direction And Candidate Integration Checklist

Make Galaxia a distributed industrial network. The recurring player decisions are where to manufacture, what to transport and which persistent installation is worth expanding. Environmental production, extraction economics, power supply and communication give locations practical value. These are product-design principles, not destination categories stored in code.

The following checklist organizes the industrial direction. It must not delay a complete progression path by requiring every destination to receive bespoke industry first. It is not blanket approval of every proposed data-processing or power detail:

| Complete player outcome | Decision owner |
| --- | --- |
| Deploy the opener, discover the system, perform an initial lunar mission and return usable resources | [[Specify Alpha Opening Mission Flow]] |
| Establish a working microgravity workshop and manufacture electronics and satellite materials from obtainable feedstock | [[Define First Physical Station Contract]], [[Design Early Orbital Production Recipe Chain]] |
| Extract crater resources, process them, recover reagents and supply useful construction or equipment | [[Design Lunar Crater Processing Recipe Chain]] |
| Keep remote factories supplied with physical freight and viable power | [[Require Explicit Interplanetary Logistics]], [[Define Space Factory Power Supply]] |
| Use communication and processed prospecting for actual remote operation and extraction benefits | [[Define Remote Android Body Lifecycle]], [[Define Prospecting Data Processing]] |
| Continue through every retained tier with reachable sources, processing, transport and downstream consumers | [[Map Alpha Tier Routes]] |

Rocket missions and androids retain their existing accepted scope. This proposal does not redesign their ongoing implementation. Persistent infrastructure may be expanded or reused across tiers. Later destinations may use the accepted complete backfill contract instead of requiring another bespoke mechanic.

Luna industry is an initial delivery milestone, not the Alpha endpoint. Modular satellites, richer research experiments and new room-discovery algorithms remain deferred. The bounded prospecting-consumer work and detailed service economy still require owner approval.

## Overall Floor Status

The full-progression-first delivery priority is accepted. Individual contracts are substantially specified, while exact release closure and remaining service/content choices still require review.

The imported proposals have now been classified by their owning review tickets. Their original authorship does not itself approve them. The project owner can choose the Alpha capability floor from accepted directions, while explicitly deferred content remains deferred. The unresolved work is selecting the required complete chains, not waiting for another generic proposal-review round.

## Required Input Before Resolution

- Use the completed imported-proposal classifications to separate accepted intent from outdated, speculative or rejected content.
- Identify which proposals express Galaxia's essential product identity and which are optional depth or post-Alpha ambitions.
- Trace each candidate mandatory capability through a complete input, player decision, consequence, and progression continuation path.
- Obtain the project owner's approval of the resulting capability floor. Preserve explicit external dependencies on individual topics rather than imposing a blanket consultation blocker.

## Decision to Produce

A short, explicit list of mandatory Galaxia-specific Alpha capabilities, each linked to the destinations and Closed Tier Cycles that prove it. Everything not on that list may be deferred or supplied through a Galacticraft-Equivalent Destination where doing so preserves the complete progression.
