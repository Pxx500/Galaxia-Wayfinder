# Native Android Batteries And GT Energy Hatches

Source: Project lead instructions in the android implementation conversation, 2026-10-22
Imported: 2026-10-22

## Source Snapshot

The owner rejected android-specific Power Core items and recursive battery crafting
The owner requested checking existing GT battery capacities and agreed that robot consumption can increase to retain the proposed endurance
The owner then accepted the native-battery proposal and requested native GregTech Energy Hatches in the structure of both machines

The accepted chassis sequence remains EV, LuV and UV
The proposed native components are one Small Sunnarium Battery at EV, one Large Sunnarium Battery at LuV and one Large Naquadria Battery at UV
The checked local GT dependency gives these capacities as 6,400,000 EU, 102,400,000 EU and 1,638,400,000 EU
The accepted EV operating proposal is 128 EU/t idle, an additional 64 EU/t walking or 192 EU/t sprinting, 640 EU per jump, 128 EU/t heating and 128 EU/t in zero gravity
LuV operating costs multiply EV by four and UV by sixteen
The charging proposal is 1,024, 16,384 and 262,144 EU/t under sufficient supplied power
Existing substantial chassis material quantities remain the manufacture basis, replacing only the previously proposed energy component with one native battery

The station should use real GT power through native Energy Hatches rather than direct controller cable injection
Cheap recipes and free power remain developer-laboratory facilities, not production recipes

The exact battery names and capacities were checked against the local GT5 dependency, version 5.09.54.64
This source records the owner's accepted direction and the numerical proposal, not a claim about every component being available in the final GTNH pack
