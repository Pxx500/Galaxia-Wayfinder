# Android Tier Spacing And Station Feedback

Source: Project lead instructions following the overnight android PR research report
Imported: 2026-10-21

## Source Snapshot

The owner rejected the conservative battery and chassis scaling in the first report
GT scales nominal voltage by four per tier, and the android progression should reflect meaningful industrial upgrades
The owner selected android chassis at EV, LuV and UV, two GT tiers apart
The proposed ingredient families were considered reasonable, but concrete substantial quantities are required so bodies are not too cheap

The owner also requested on-screen feedback when a player or android enters the correct place inside a physical station
The example was a charging connection message on entry and a disconnection message on exit
The requested placement resembles the existing return-to-body hint near the bottom of the screen

Current charging is restricted to an inactive android in its original dock
The feedback implementation therefore distinguishes a connected charging port from energy actually entering an actively controlled body
The request does not itself establish active-body charging, shared-dock charging or new recovery rules

A revised numerical proposal is retained at `~/.agents/docs/Galaxia/worktrees/remote-android-bodies/docs/research/2026-10-21-android-tier-scaling-revision.md`
Its specific quantities, Power Core items, 1/16/256 million EU capacities, operating costs and process powers remain proposals
They have not been accepted merely by being recorded here
