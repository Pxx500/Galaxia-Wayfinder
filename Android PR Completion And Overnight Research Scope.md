# Android PR Completion And Overnight Research Scope

Source: Project lead instructions in the remote android implementation chat
Imported: 2026-10-21

## Source Snapshot

The owner established three requirements for closing the android PR

1. Research player inventory size, the consequences for other mods, whether changing it is worth the cost, and whether preserving the inventory while locking selected slots is an alternative
2. Add tiered androids, with a discussion of which statistics or bonuses should vary by tier
3. Add real production recipes while preserving artificial recipes for the manual laboratory

The owner then went to sleep and requested a report ready for the morning
The report should answer all three points and propose additional ways to make androids fit Galaxia, including original ideas
Ideas must include consequences, implementation feasibility and concrete integration points, rather than concept names alone

The requirements above do not select an inventory capacity, number of chassis tiers, exact statistics, battery architecture or recipe bill
The report is research and design input, not authorization to silently settle those choices or expand implementation scope
