# Repo Source - Vael Star System Notes

Source: repository working notes `AI/Current/VaelStarSystem.md`
Imported: 2026-08-01

Implementation fields and registry migration steps from the original document were intentionally discarded. This snapshot preserves the candidate fiction, established progression constraints, body-role requirements, and unresolved canon decisions.

## Source Snapshot

### Established Direction

- A Star Map object may exist without a player-accessible dimension.
- A playable dimension is one possible gameplay representation of a celestial object, not a second identity for it.
- Non-playable bodies can still support hierarchy, lore, orbital presentation, satellites, stations, transfer planning, anchors, and future mechanics.
- The established early progression route is `Overworld -> Moon -> Mars`.
- `Overworld` is the starting home world, `Moon` is the first major offworld target, and `Mars` is the first planet beyond it.
- `Viridis`, `Calx`, and `Dunia` are not current early-body names unless reused as lore, regions, ruins, anomalies, or historical names.

Older names such as `Perihelia`, `Tenebrae`, `Theia`, `Hemateria`, and `Anamnesis` remain candidate lore, regions, anomalies, historical names, or special objects rather than accepted planets.

### Per-Body Requirements

Every retained body should eventually define:

- a one-line fictional identity and visual character;
- a meaningful gameplay or progression role;
- its parent and broad orbital relationship;
- whether and how players can access it;
- principal hazards, resources, and environmental constraints;
- satellite, station, outpost, automation, or logistics roles where applicable;
- the reason the body must remain on the Star Map.

### Open Decisions

- Which candidate bodies and names are canonical for Alpha.
- Which older names become lore or special objects and which are retired.
- Which bodies require playable spaces and which remain remote Star Map objects.
- Which gas giants need moon or ring gameplay rather than acting only as anchors.
- Whether orbital distance should usually track progression difficulty; the current leaning allows hard inner worlds such as Spire to unlock late.
- What the `Overworld -> Moon -> Mars` route teaches, requires, and unlocks.


## Classification

- The separation between Star Map objects and dimensions was integrated into the active Wayfinder.
- The requirement for every retained object to provide meaningful gameplay was integrated into the active Wayfinder.
- `Overworld -> Moon -> Mars` is accepted as the mandatory early progression baseline.
- An orbital station between the Overworld and the Moon remains a possible later addition.
- The custom Vael roster, names, lore, and body roles are deferred until the originating developer returns or the team deliberately resumes that work.
- Active Alpha planning should use the current GTNH destination roster as its working baseline.
- Older candidate names remain only in this source snapshot and are discarded from active planning.
