# Project Lead Remote Android Bodies Proposal

Source: Project lead's direct proposal in the Wayfinder conversation
Imported: 2026-10-18

## Source Snapshot

The player could manufacture robotic android bodies and control them remotely while their biological body remains elsewhere. For example, the biological body stays on Overworld while a multiblock on Luna produces an android.

- A suitable satellite connection between the biological body's location and the android's location is required. Remote control reserves part of the available communication bandwidth.
- The android has its own inventory and body state rather than bringing the biological body's inventory with the player.
- The robotic body uses electrical energy.
- Loss of the required bandwidth interrupts the connection. The exact disconnected-body behavior was not specified.
- The android may operate only in the dimension containing its associated docking multiblock.
- Attempting to leave by Rocket permanently destroys the android and ends remote control. Its manufacturing materials are lost.
- The proposal is intended to reduce repeated personal travel without replacing resource freight. Rockets would still carry supplies and support establishing access to new places.

The proposal did not select an Alpha milestone, technology tier, exact bandwidth or energy costs, biological-body protection, remote-body death and inventory recovery, ownership rules, or treatment of other dimension-transfer mechanisms. It is not an implementation instruction or an approved complete lifecycle.
