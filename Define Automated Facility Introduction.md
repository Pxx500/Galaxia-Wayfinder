# Automated Facility Introduction

## Introduction

EV is the accepted working balance target for the first Automated Facilities. Earlier player-operated exploration and physical production supply the prerequisites. Access follows construction costs and the player's ability to produce the required parts, not a player-tier flag or a disabled build button.

Space-Grade Alloy is the primary structural material for both Automated Stations and Automated Outposts, with smaller specialist-component costs. Exact bills of materials remain open. This direction was accepted on 2026-10-23 and has not completed balance validation.

## Established Input

This stage concerns Star Map-managed Automated Facilities, not ordinary automation with physical GT machines. Earlier gameplay is governed by [[Define Player Presence and Automation Tradeoffs]].

Galaxia materials supply construction prerequisites without requiring a Physical Station specifically. [[Design Lunar Crater Processing Recipe Chain]] owns the accepted Space-Grade Alloy manufacturing recipes and Facility construction-material direction.

## Gameplay Flow

The player explores and builds physical production to obtain the parts needed for an Automated Facility. Once they can produce its construction materials and specialist components, they can build an Automated Station or Outpost and manage it through the Star Map.
