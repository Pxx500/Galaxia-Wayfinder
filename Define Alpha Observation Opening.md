# Define Alpha Observation Opening

## Question

How does Galaxia Alpha first expose the Star Map and celestial discovery to the player?

## Resolution

The Star Map begins unavailable. The team launches a Rocket from its Silo to deploy an ordinary Prospecting Satellite into the starting world's orbit. Successful deployment activates the Star Map and exposes the objects whose initial knowledge is authored in their celestial definitions.

Alpha uses one observation capability level and one hidden-object detection level. Deployment itself does not reveal hidden objects or their resources. The same Prospecting Satellite continues normal discovery and detailed scans afterwards. A deployed observation satellite is permanent for Alpha and has no maintenance, failure, destruction, or replacement lifecycle.

[[Specify Alpha Opening Mission Flow]] owns the accepted pre-map local launch flow, payload, initial knowledge boundary, and remaining vehicle-return question.

## Implementation Status

The current direct Galactic Map item opening path does not enforce the accepted first-satellite prerequisite. Current entry-point evidence is recorded in Specify Alpha Opening Mission Flow. This is implementation work under the resolved decision, not a new question about whether the satellite is required.
