# Alpha Observation Opening

## Accepted Decision

The Star Map starts locked. Successfully deploying the Primitive Telescope Satellite from the Silo into Overworld orbit grants the team launch achievement and opens the team's map. Local orbital deployment works without opening the map first.

The map reveals objects whose starting visibility is authored in celestial definitions. Only Overworld resources start known. Luna is already a known object with unknown resources. Possessing the achievement provides passive Luna celestial-profile survey progress. Scan completion reveals knowledge, not the launch achievement. This never scans physical Minecraft blocks or deposits.

The opener is a scripted payload and achievement mission, not an unrestricted Prospecting Satellite or a required managed orbital asset. It needs no ordinary satellite worker, maintenance or failure lifecycle.

## Gameplay Flow

The team launches the Primitive Telescope Satellite from the Silo into Overworld orbit. Successful deployment grants the launch achievement and opens the Star Map, even though the map was locked before the launch.

The player can see authored starting objects, including Luna, but only Overworld resources are initially known. The achievement provides passive Luna profile survey progress, and completing the survey reveals knowledge rather than granting the launch achievement.
