# Android Dock Visualization And Tiering Input

Source: Project lead conversation in the remote android implementation chat
Imported: 2026-10-20

## Source Snapshot

The owner found the dock's animated arrow visually bland and requested a full android outline that gradually builds from the feet to the head, row by row, following manufacturing progress
The arrow may remain, but it should not be the main manufacturing visualization
A screenshot also shows the blue arrow fill painting the left border before reaching its interior

The owner requested options for the android production entry tier, crafting materials, tiered chassis statistics and battery capacities
The dock should manufacture different androids according to the materials supplied to its input inventory

For repair, the owner suggested consuming materials when an android is below full health
An example was selecting one of six materials and restoring a fixed amount such as two hearts per consumed item
This was presented as an idea to discuss, not a finalized repair algorithm or balance value

No exact tier list, final recipes, battery capacities or repair selection rule was accepted in this conversation yet
The existing resolved lifecycle note retains its provisional EV introduction target
