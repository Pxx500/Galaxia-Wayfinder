# Alpha Design Map

[[00 - Galaxia Alpha Command Center|Browse by system]]

## Destination

A playable Galaxia Alpha with complete routes through the retained GTNH progression and automatable infrastructure. Establish those routes before adding bespoke mechanics to every destination.

Environmental and resource rules may leave only one suitable location. Alternative routes are useful when supported by the game, not a quota.
