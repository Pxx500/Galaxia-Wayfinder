# Galaxia Research Proposal Research

> Historical or reference record. Read dated outcomes and later corrections before using earlier proposals. This record is not an active instruction to execute its plan, and its code or verification claims have not been revalidated by the 2026-09-10 vault consolidation.

## Provenance and Governing Constraint

Source: [[Discord Source - Research Proposal]]  
Original: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wZDwmi78wMBlzFNc08W2tMrgyYfpygY0DEIyXy-Rqvo/edit?tab=t.0

This is an unaccepted developer proposal. Its purpose is to make planetary exploration meaningful through advancement-like objectives and research milestones.

The accepted Alpha rule from Define Non-Material Tier Gate Role governs this review:

- every Ore Tier currently retains an Ore-Backed Tier Gate;
- research may enrich or gate a concrete destination, investigation, activity, or capability;
- Alpha does not require research in every tier and does not assume research-only or data-only progression;
- research mechanics should be added when their gameplay purpose is understood, not merely to make every gate composite.

Ore placement is outside this review and remains governed by the maintained Ore Sheet.

## Superseded or Rejected Claims

- Alpha does not accept a universal rule that every planet requires `100` Research before normal progression can continue.
- Research is not a replacement for the ore-backed material hard gate and does not make an Ore Tier research-only or data-only.
- Exact point values, milestone spacing, and the `0–400` range are not balance facts.
- The proposal's sample objectives are examples, not a destination checklist or implicit Alpha content commitment.
- `Research Station` must not be assumed to mean Physical Station, Automated Station, Automated Outpost, or generic Automated Facility until its placement and player-access contract are selected.

## Genuine Decisions Still Needed

1. Does Alpha require one objective-based Research loop anywhere, or are satellite-data and destination-dependent production sufficient for its non-extraction floor?
2. If objective Research is retained, is progress a continuous score, discrete findings, authored milestones, or another representation?
3. May Research gate a specific production line or destination action, and which concrete Closed Tier Cycle justifies that gate?
4. Are Research objectives team-shared, player-specific, or asset-specific?
5. How are procedural objectives kept achievable across seeds, regenerated terrain, multiplayer teams, and already-explored worlds?
6. Is a Research Station required, and if so, is it player-accessible infrastructure, an Automated Facility, or merely a capability installed in either?
7. Which rewards remain optional mastery bonuses without becoming mandatory GTNH optimization?

## Deferred or Outside the Current Alpha Floor

- Hundreds of objectives per planet.
- Completionist trophies and a formal `400`-point endpoint.
- Rover-specific objective families unless a Rover becomes part of an accepted destination contract.
- A universal Research system spanning every destination before one complete representative loop has been validated.
- Final UI resembling Minecraft Advancements before the underlying ownership, scoring, and persistence model is chosen.

## Provenance-Only Rationale

The references to Minecraft Advancements, Satisfactory hard drives and combat, Stardew Valley's Community Center, randomizers, and routing checks explain the author's design influences. They are useful inspiration but do not add product requirements.

## Recommended Routing

- Use the proposal primarily as input to Define Alpha Research and Production Floor.
- Route satellite and telescope examples through the accepted celestial-knowledge and prospecting-data tickets rather than creating a parallel Research-data model.
- Route Station-based examples to Define First Physical Station Contract without assuming that Research is the Station's required pre-Mars capability.
- Preserve the environmental and biological objective families as candidate destination-specific gameplay, not as a universal scoring mandate.
