# Define Alpha Research and Production Floor

## Question

Which research, satellite-data, destination-dependent production, and other non-extraction gameplay chains must appear somewhere in Galaxia Alpha, beyond the already accepted acquisition and processing contracts?

## Established Decisions

- [[Define Alpha Technology Knowledge Contract]] owns consumed Technology Blueprints, team knowledge, production checks, and catalog behavior. Those rules are resolved, not blocked by the remaining proposal review.
- [[Define Prospecting Data Processing]] owns the accepted origin-bound processing and return pipeline. [[Define Data Processor Machine Topology]] owns the resolved expandable-multiblock design.
- Define Non-Material Tier Gate Role excludes mandatory bosses and dungeons from progression.
- Define First Physical Station Contract retains orbital-production candidates and a working lunar Space-Grade Alloy direction. These do not yet prove the complete mandatory production floor.
- Richer planetary experiments are later work, not a prerequisite for the accepted Alpha blueprint mechanism.

## Production Machinery Baseline

The owner reaffirmed the existing project direction on 2026-09-17: Galaxia primarily uses GregTech machines and their normal production capabilities. A custom machine is an explicit exception justified by a process requirement that existing machinery cannot satisfy, not a default choice for each new chain.

Apply this baseline to orbital production and other Galaxia processing chains. Investigate existing capabilities before proposing a custom machine. Do not ask the owner to choose between GT and custom machinery again without identifying the concrete unmet requirement.

## Accepted Orbital Production Minimum

On 2026-09-16 the owner initially accepted one complete orbital production chain as the Alpha minimum. On 2026-09-17 they expanded that requirement to both silicon-germanium crystals and specialty fluoride optical fibers because satellite and control-electronics progression will use them. Each chain must run from obtainable feedstock to a product with a concrete use. More orbital production is planned later.

[[Define First Physical Station Contract]] owns the environmental rationale and early Rocket-control technology role. Both material families are required. Their first product allocation is resolved below, while exact recipes remain open. [[Define Satellite Manufacturing and Deployment Progression]] owns satellite use and deployment.

## Accepted Material Allocation

On 2026-10-07 the owner assigned specialty fluoride optical fibers to satellite production. Silicon-germanium crystals supply electronics for both Rockets and satellites. The first Rocket automation/control-electronics upgrade therefore uses SiGe under this allocation. The earlier recommendation to require both orbital material families in that first upgrade is superseded.

Both production chains remain part of Alpha and require microgravity. This allocation does not add orbital-only final assembly or alter the cheaper opening satellite's starting-world recipe contract. Concrete component recipes and quantities remain content design.

The owner requested a germanium acquisition audit on 2026-09-17. After the verified IV Alloy Blast Smelter route was reported, they chose to retain silicon-germanium and add a Galaxia low-yield sphalerite-to-germanium route at an early stage, with HV as the working target. The existing ABS route remains a later, more attractive production method. This explicitly authorizes a new early acquisition route, not lowering the ABS recipe's tier or replacing its existing processing chain.

## Early Germanium Bootstrap

- Use sphalerite to obtain small quantities of germanium before the ordinary ABS route becomes available.
- HV is the owner's proposed early target, matching the opening space stage. Inputs and machine requirements must actually fit that stage rather than relying on a later-tier reagent or component.
- Preserve a meaningful reason to upgrade to the later ABS method. [[Design Early Orbital Production Recipe Chain]] records the selected working 8 mB early yield and its comparison with the unchanged ABS route. Remaining balance work does not reopen the choice of a separate early acquisition route.
- Use existing GT machinery and normal recipe maps under the production baseline. No custom machine is selected by the phrase custom acquisition route.
- Retain the SiGe crystal direction. The precise early recipe, material form and other orbital feedstocks still require content design.

The owner requested a consumer audit of germanium and its derived materials before choosing the acquisition direction. The later choice of a separate low-yield route is recorded above. Earlier access must still be checked against unrelated GTNH progression during implementation. No reduction of the existing ABS tier or change to its recipes has been approved merely because germanium is obscure.

## Remaining Decisions

[[Design Early Orbital Production Recipe Chain]] owns the accepted SiGe seed cycle, fluoride thermal flow with four microgravity operations, revised satellites and Solar Cell, Solar Glass Sheet and Space Grade Solar Panel recipes. It distinguishes selected working recipes from remaining proposals. The owner clarified on 2026-10-08 that the new solar content consists only of recipe-component items. Placeable generators, EU output and their operating rules are outside the current chain scope. The new material route must preserve existing GTNH solar balance.

Final satellite assembly need not happen in orbit. Exact chemistry and early feedstock availability still require verification. The two-chain Alpha requirement is settled, not another choice between the material families.

SiGe crystals and fluoride optical fibers require microgravity, not an orbital-location flag. Space-Grade Alloy requires absence of atmosphere without a gravity restriction. [[Define Celestial Capability Rules]] owns this shared distinction, including valid non-orbital environments.

- Which additional destination-dependent production chains are essential to Alpha rather than optional depth?
- Which reviewed proposals supply those chains and satisfy a complete input, activity, output, and progression continuation?
- The concrete feedstocks, processing and component recipes for both accepted orbital material families under their resolved product allocation. Machinery follows the production baseline above unless a concrete unmet requirement justifies an explicit exception.
- Which concrete technologies and recipe groups populate the accepted knowledge mechanism? Material and transport placement is developed in Map Alpha Tier Routes.

[[Review Crater Nodes Proposal]] resolves the revised crater gameplay chain. Concrete recipes, balance and integration remain execution work. The imported-proposal review is also resolved, so the remaining production-floor discussion is unblocked. None of this reopens the accepted technology acquisition contract.

## Consolidation

On 2026-09-12 the settled technology mechanism was extracted into its own resolved decision. This ticket retains its original broader content-floor question and is not marked complete merely because the blueprint mechanism is settled.
