# Define Alpha Research and Production Floor

## Question

Which research, satellite-data, destination-dependent production, and other non-extraction gameplay chains must appear somewhere in Galaxia Alpha, beyond the already accepted acquisition and processing contracts?

## Established Decisions

- [[Define Alpha Technology Knowledge Contract]] owns consumed Technology Blueprints, team knowledge, production checks, and catalog behavior. Those rules are resolved, not blocked by the remaining proposal review.
- [[Define Prospecting Data Processing]] owns the accepted origin-bound processing and return pipeline. Its physical machine topology remains with its own feedback and design tickets.
- Define Non-Material Tier Gate Role excludes mandatory bosses and dungeons from progression.
- Define First Physical Station Contract retains orbital-production candidates and a working lunar Space-Grade Alloy direction. These do not yet prove the complete mandatory production floor.
- Richer planetary experiments are later work, not a prerequisite for the accepted Alpha blueprint mechanism.

## Remaining Decisions

- Which additional destination-dependent production chains are essential to Alpha rather than optional depth?
- Which reviewed proposals supply those chains and satisfy a complete input, activity, output, and progression continuation?
- Which concrete technologies and recipe groups populate the accepted knowledge mechanism? Material and transport placement is developed in Map Alpha Tier Routes.

Crater-specific choices still require designer reconciliation. That blocker does not reopen the technology acquisition contract or prevent discussion of unrelated resolved systems.

## Consolidation

On 2026-09-12 the settled technology mechanism was extracted into its own resolved decision. This ticket retains its original broader content-floor question and is not marked complete merely because the blueprint mechanism is settled.
