# Personal Dimension Homeworld Integration

## Personal Dimensions

Galaxia Alpha does not support launching from or returning to GTNH Personal Dimensions.

A Personal Dimension portal may be placed on another celestial body. Treating the Personal Dimension as an Overworld launch origin or remembered return point would therefore create a teleportation path between celestial destinations and bypass Galaxia's explicit interplanetary logistics.

Personal Dimensions are not Star Map Objects and receive no special homeworld or launch-origin integration in Alpha.

## Gameplay Flow

Players can place a Personal Dimension portal on a celestial body, but cannot launch from or return to the Personal Dimension through Galaxia. Travel between celestial destinations remains subject to Galaxia's interplanetary logistics.
