# GTNH Chemical Balance Reference

Imported: 2026-10-11

## Source Snapshot

The article describes recipe-balance conventions for materials with chemical formulas. Its aim is internal consistency, not strict real-world chemistry. Recipe inputs and outputs normally balance their components, though intentionally smaller outputs are permitted.

For solids, A₂B₃C represents six full compound dusts, equivalent to two A dusts, three B dusts, and one C dust. Each constituent fluid uses 1,000 units instead of one dust. For fluids, 1,000 units of A₂B fluid corresponds to 2,000 units of A and 1,000 of B. Solid components use one dust per 1,000 fluid units. A molten material with a solid counterpart follows the solid rule. A bracketed compound counts as one component: A₂(complex formula)₃ is five full dusts, equivalent to two A dusts and three complex-compound dusts.

### Owner clarification

Undefined mixtures such as scandium-rich bauxite have no fixed formula-derived chemical-balance coefficients. Their concentrations and extraction yields can be authored and tuned. Do not present those choices as consequences of an invented chemical formula.
