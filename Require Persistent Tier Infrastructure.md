# Require Persistent Tier Infrastructure

Resolved: 2026-07-31

## Infrastructure Requirement

As the working Alpha rule, every Ore Tier requires the use of at least one piece of **Persistent Tier Infrastructure** within its Closed Tier Cycle. The infrastructure need not be located on a planetary surface and need not take the form of a conventional player base.

The exact infrastructure is defined per tier. It may be newly constructed, upgraded, or reused from an earlier tier where that produces a meaningful continuing dependency. Whether reuse is appropriate is a tier-content decision rather than a global requirement.

## Eligible Forms

Persistent Tier Infrastructure may include, depending on the destination and mechanic:

- a surface installation or production base;
- an orbital station;
- an automated outpost;
- a persistent satellite or observation network;
- a logistics, transport, or cargo installation in space;
- another durable celestial asset that continues participating in the tier's progression chain after initial deployment.

Temporary mission equipment, a one-time landing, or an extraction visit that leaves no continuing capability does not satisfy the rule by itself.

## Exception Policy

- The rule is the default for all Ore Tiers during Alpha charting.
- Do not invent exceptions preemptively.
- If a later tier design has a concrete reason why persistent infrastructure would harm its gameplay, add and document a narrow exception for that tier.
- An exception changes only the named tier or route; it does not weaken the default rule elsewhere.

## Consequences

- Each tier portfolio must identify which persistent infrastructure capability participates in every Valid Tier Route.
- The infrastructure may live on a surface, in orbit, or elsewhere in space.
- Infrastructure must have a continuing input, output, capability, maintenance role, logistics role, or downstream use so that it does not become an Orphaned Mechanic.
- Alpha may implement a shallower version of a planned infrastructure system, but it must still close the tier's cause-and-effect chain.
- A tier that can be completed entirely through disposable travel and ore extraction fails the working Alpha rule unless it has an explicit exception.

## Gameplay Flow

The player uses a durable installation or other persistent asset as part of each tier's progression chain. It may be new, upgraded or reused when it provides a meaningful continuing dependency. A temporary visit or disposable extraction trip alone does not meet the default rule.
