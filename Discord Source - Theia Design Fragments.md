Theia
This document aims to guide the progression design of Theia (the moon ingame)


Key themes: 


Quiet and desolate
Desolation has preserved the shared magic of the moon and the earth far better than on earth
Perfectly preserved time capsule of the solar system




List of potential environmental factors on Theia:

Radiation (notable lack of magnetosphere)
Microgravity
Terrain
“Moon bugs” (this was a major fear scientists had about the moon)
Effects of magic
Soft sand/dust of a kind (see spirit rover)
Blood moon
No global magnetic field
Patchy magnetic fields
Craters have stronger magnetic fields
Dust




Potential Theia features:

Craters with select material deposits from the objects that impacted it
