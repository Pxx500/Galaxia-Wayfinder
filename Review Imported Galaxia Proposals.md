# Review Imported Galaxia Proposals

## Question

Which concrete planet, research, production, and progression proposals in the isolated Discord source snapshots still represent current developer intent, which are outdated or disputed, and which require confirmation from their responsible designers before they may influence Galaxia Alpha?

## Resolution

On 2026-09-16 all five prerequisite proposal reviews have recorded resolutions. This aggregate review is closed by consolidating those existing classifications, not by accepting all imported content.

- [[Review Galaxia Main Planning Document]] owns the classification of the main document, including excluded ore claims and deprecated sections.
- [[Review Galaxia Dev Notes]] separates corroborated systems from rejected or historical progression and implementation ideas.
- [[Review Research Proposal]] preserves useful activity-design principles without accepting a universal research-score gate. Later owner decisions place Alpha technology acquisition in the consumed-blueprint contract.
- [[Review Theia Design Fragments]] keeps Theia-specific lore and bespoke mechanics deferred. Resolution of the crater proposal does not accept unrelated Theia content.
- [[Review Crater Nodes Proposal]] records the owner's revised Alpha crater-extraction, fluid-processing, scanner and bulk-alloy direction, superseding conflicting source suggestions.

The remaining Alpha capability and production-floor questions may now proceed. They must use these classifications and their owning decisions, not infer blanket approval from the imported sources.
